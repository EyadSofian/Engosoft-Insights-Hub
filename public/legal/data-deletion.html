Data deletion instructions — Engosoft Attribution

Engosoft Attribution is Engosoft's internal advertising-measurement app. It records which Meta ad started a conversation or produced a lead, so Engosoft can see which advertising works.

What the app stores

  • Message and lead identifiers issued by Meta
  • The Facebook Page, Instagram account or WhatsApp number that received the message
  • The ad, ad set, campaign and creative identifiers from the ad referral
  • The time of the message or lead

The app does not store message text, attachments, names, profile pictures, phone numbers, email addresses or form answers.

How long it is kept

Attribution evidence is deleted automatically after 365 days.

How to request deletion

  1. Send a request through engosoft.com/contact-us with the subject Engosoft Attribution data deletion.
  2. Include the Facebook, Instagram or WhatsApp account you used to contact Engosoft, and the approximate date.
  3. Engosoft deletes the related attribution records within 30 days and confirms by email.

You can also remove Engosoft's access at any time in your Facebook settings under Apps and websites.
