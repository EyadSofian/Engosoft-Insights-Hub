# Six-month lead-to-paid report: requirement audit

Reviewed 2026-10-25. Page: `/leads?view=performance`. The six cohorts are calendar entry months; the current month is partial. Recommendation, WhatsApp Broadcast (including the observed Odoo typo `Whtsapp Broadcast`), and AutoDialer/AutoDailer Broadcast are excluded by the shared acquisition source/segment policy. An em dash means evidence is unavailable, not zero.

The classification panel now separates three independent axes: current CRM type/status (Active Lead, Lost Lead, Open/Won/Lost Opportunity), current Odoo `Lead Segment` value (including Fresh and blank), and post-creation Yeastar PBX contact evidence. These are never treated as one funnel stage. The "Before CRM" tab deliberately shows unavailable rather than zero: Odoo cannot count people who never entered Odoo. An external event feed with person-level reconciliation is required. The top Leads navigation also exposes a Sales closing channels tab (`/leads?view=channels`) based on Odoo `Closing Won Channel`, and a Lost cohort tab (`/leads?view=lost`) with platform/source/course/salesperson/media-buyer breakdowns and separate Lost Lead vs Lost Opportunity counts. The full `/lost` analysis remains linked. Closing channel is not the acquisition source.

Current Lost counts can be inspected by course, platform/channel, source, current salesperson and attributed media buyer. Each row's Lost percentage is `current Lost / eligible new CRM records in that row`, with Lost Leads and Lost Opportunities separate. Platform uses the Odoo source label, not an independently verified ad-platform click. These are **entry-month cohort outcomes as of today**, not a historical month-end snapshot or a count of all records that moved Lost during the month.

## What data is still required for a complete evaluation

1. Immutable Lead Segment/Fresh classification at creation, or an Odoo event history from which the initial class can be reconstructed. Current `Lead Segment` does not answer what the field was at entry.
2. Consistent manual QA verdicts and reason codes on sampled/failed leads, especially to distinguish invalid customer details from poor course fit.
3. Opportunity stage-transition timestamps if management needs actual conversion rate from one sales stage to the next or stage aging in month 1/month 2.
4. `sale.order.opportunity_id` (or another audited lead key) on sales orders currently missing it. An invoice's seller identifies the seller, not the originating lead, source, campaign or media buyer.
5. A bulk, attributable Chatter/WhatsApp event feed if all contact attempts—not just Yeastar PBX—must be scored. Until then, the outreach measure is explicitly PBX-only.

The page and workbook expose the limits next to the numbers. They must not be removed merely to produce a single headline conversion rate.
